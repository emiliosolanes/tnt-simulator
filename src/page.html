Illustrative storyline. One temperature-controlled container from a cold store in Duisburg by rail to Rotterdam, by sea through Algeciras to New York, by rail to Chicago and by truck to the consignee, then back empty. Not a claim that any carrier publishes every event. Places are generic; company names are left out. Senders, forwarders and receivers in the ten key moments are typical roles, shown for illustration. Real trackers and reefer units report far more often than the one reading per leg shown here.

Event types and names follow the DCSA Track and Trace standard: Shipment, Transport, Equipment, IoT and Reefer.
